## The representation that succeeded on the matched family

The working space is the real Hermitian CAR coefficient algebra, restricted by exact symmetries and quotiented by the specified fixed-number ideal. Positive squares support an energy bound through

\[
H-bI=\sum_j p_j^\dagger p_j+(\widehat N-N)X+R,
\qquad E_0\ge b-\sum_w |R_w|.
\]

For the matched family, the local charge-preserving algebra has basis 1, nL, nR, T, T†, and nLnR. Its flavor-permutation, global-swap, and real-Hermitian bookkeeping gives **39 coefficient directions** through body weight four for every number of matched flavors m ≥ 4. The truncated number ideal removes **19 directions**, leaving a **20-dimensional coefficient quotient**. The derivation is in [the dimension note](marginal_invariant_dimension.md). This does not establish a universal 20-coordinate representation of matter or a simple positivity boundary.

On the ten-mode test, full Reynolds averaging reduced 1,531 equations and 115,221 PSD scalar variables to **39 equations and 2,082 PSD scalar variables**. The largest PSD block fell from 186 to 14. Assembly took 23.22 seconds; the reduced SDP solve took 1.15 seconds, excluding export/replay. See [the Reynolds result](marginal_reynolds_results.md).

The subsequent generator-based coefficient projection avoids factorial group enumeration in that step and retains 39 rows at 8, 10, 12, and 14 modes. Stabilizer construction and Gram export still enumerate the full group. Symmetry reduction itself is established methodology; see [Gatermann and Parrilo](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211450). These experiments supply a particular fermionic construction and exact receipts, not a new general symmetry theorem.

The fuller proof is approximately 1.8 MB. Its older [distilled 36-orbit certificate](marginal_distillation_results.md) is approximately 340 KB and contains 2,708 seed coefficients. Those directions were extracted after the full SDP. A separate, newer chain now discovers directions from a cubic starting point and compresses them into 21 orbits, without that full quartic input.

## What failed, and what remains

The earlier seven-step adaptive run did not improve the energy. A longer symmetry-reduced run now succeeds using the same basic negative-eigenvector principle. Under a common 117-cut budget, energy lookahead gave width 9.928e-7, single most-negative pricing gave 8.157e-7, and fixed batches gave 1.031e-3. Thus lookahead has no established advantage in this comparison. The earlier failure was not a general failure of spectral selection. [New comparison and exact receipts](marginal_energy_selector_results.md).

The central unresolved task is **to discover a small set of energy-relevant positive squares directly from the Hamiltonian, with controllable error and tractable cost as the system grows**. Direct discovery and compression now succeed on the ten-mode matched case, using 311 cubic PSD scalar variables and 117 added scalar weights. The method still constructs complete quartic candidate dictionaries and depends on matched-model symmetry. Its success does not prove a general automatic representation with controlled large-system cost. Exact equality at the physical optimum, uniform accuracy across larger particle counts, and general molecular scaling have not been established.

An optional [stabilizer pricing path](marginal_reduced_pricing_results.md) now performs candidate eigensolves in blocks no larger than 14, with a measured roughly fivefold reduction in isolated pricing time. Complete small-model spectra and ten-mode negative directions agree with full pricing to numerical precision. Its 117-cut run certifies width 1.823e-6; the default full pricing run remains tighter at the same budget. This is not evidence of faster complete discovery or elimination of full coefficient-map construction.

The checker also now combines averaged orbit squares with unaveraged local squares.
