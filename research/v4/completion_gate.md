Decision: **the broad goal is not complete**.

A read-only reviewing agent independently recomputed the means from exact rational certificate risks and checked saved certificates on evaluation rows 0, 15 and 31, including the signed-moment, cumulative and conventional-batch methods. The sampled certificates passed. Across all 32 cases the cumulative mean risk is 0.3955255627292014, exactly equal to the frozen first-stage method. The exact planner's mean is 0.391406457786399. The cumulative method therefore has positive regret and no second-stage increment.

This supports a null result for exploratory supervised policy distillation. It does not support model acquisition from raw observations: the full response table and candidate grammar are supplied. Source-model applicability and physical noise calibration are assumptions.

V2 already supplies a valid bounded result in its admitted product-response family: sequential noisy acquisition, unused-control transfer, complementarity and fixed-horizon amortized savings. It remains the strongest positive compounding theorem in this workspace. V3 provides a supplied-model representation bound, not learned representation discovery.

The missing obligation is a positive resource-accounted result on at least two fresh stages where a representation or experimental operation is acquired from data and reused in subsequent discovery. Stateful conventional comparisons must share assumptions and access; a tie with an equivalent learning algorithm is permissible and must be reported. No algorithm can be required to beat all emulators of itself.
