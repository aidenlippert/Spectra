# Joint certificate discovery from a bare Hamiltonian

The new constructor recovered the H6 complement bound −6.264 from only the Hamiltonian, mode count and particle count. It generated both the charge-metric basis and the positivity dictionary. No coefficients or selected atom directions from an earlier certificate entered discovery. Both the unreduced and spin-exchange-reduced runs passed the existing rational polynomial verifier.

## Mathematical search

Write q_i=n_{iα}+n_{iβ}−1 and D=Σ_i q_i²/2. The metric is

v(q)=D(q) Σ_j c_j f_j(q),

where the f_j are generated reflection-tied charge monomials of total degree at most four. Charges satisfy q_i³=q_i. Thus v has charge degree at most six and vanishes on the valence space. Its coefficients are free optimization variables. Reflection tying restricts the search; it does not assert that every input Hamiltonian has spatial reflection symmetry.

For the supplied Hamiltonian and threshold γ, the existing compiler constructs the linear numerator K_γ(v). The joint LP asks for

v−ε_w ∈ C,     K_γ(v)−ε_K ∈ C,     E_Q[v]=1,

where C is generated by nonsingleton occupation indicators of degree at most six and (D−1) times occupation indicators of degree at most four, modulo the two fixed-spin number identities. These localizers are nonnegative on Q, where D≥1. Both positivity decompositions and the metric are solved together.

All coordinates are exact fixed-number polynomial quotient coordinates before conversion to a numerical proposal matrix. Atom completion counts use binomial formulas. Mean normalization uses exact monomial means on fixed-number slices. Since every metric feature vanishes on P, E_Q[v]=d/(d−1) E_sector[v], where d=C(m,p) for either spin species in this equal-population setting.

## Implementation and measured size

`experiments/marginal_joint_coefficient_constructor.py` provides generation, cached solving, and rational export. The input and cached proposal are bound by a canonical SHA-256 digest. Export reconstructs bounded-degree number identities and replays the full certificate. Floating solver success alone never establishes acceptance.

For the H6 input at γ=−783/125:

- 89 generated charge-feature orbits reduce to 48 independent metric directions.
- The quotient has 400 coordinates, equal to the dimension of the full fixed-spin function space for this small system.
- The generated dictionary has 96,418 columns: 86,509 positive indicators and 9,909 charge localizers.
- The joint LP has 801 equality rows, 192,885 columns and 11,327,245 nonzeros.
- Preparation took about 120–124 seconds in the recorded runs.

These dimensions are a measured discovery bottleneck. They do not demonstrate compression of the full H6 function space, and the current budgets do not establish molecular scaling beyond this case.

## Diagnostics and acceptance status

The first SciPy-bundled HiGHS 1.2.0 IPM attempt timed out. A second run without presolve failed numerically after two IPM iterations. Native HiGHS 1.15.1 dual simplex then reached its 180-second limit without a valid solution. Native IPM reached 12 iterations in 120 seconds, with improving residuals but no accepted candidate at that limit. These outcomes do not prove infeasibility.

An independent comparison with the old vanishing-metric proof checked every positive and charge atom label: all are present in the newly generated dictionary, and none of the old positive atoms is a singleton. The old metric has exact mean_Q=2375000001/2375000000, essentially one. Its normalized certified weight floor is approximately 0.000999312000579 and its numerator floor approximately 0.01369985018145. The diagnostic divides the integer polynomial coefficients by their 2×10^9 denominator. These inherited values are used only for diagnosis; they are not fed into discovery.

With a 360-second solve budget, native IPM converged in 203.66 seconds at requested metric floor 0.0009 and numerator floor 0.0001. Exact export accepted a metric floor of 0.000895411979 and numerator floor about 0.0000840349208676 after accounting for the full coefficient residual. The exported weight proof has 348 positive indicators and 52 charge localizers; the numerator has 327 and 26 respectively. Each has 1,364 number-multiplier terms. The independently replayed complement bound is −783/125.

## Verified spin-exchange reduction

`experiments/marginal_joint_spinflip.py` reconstructs the quotient basis and derives its involution by exchanging adjacent alpha/beta occupation bits. It recompiles all 48 metric directions and checks exact invariance of each weight and numerator polynomial. It also checks the cached coefficient matrix against these regenerated columns and checks the entire matrix's spin-exchange equivariance. A failed symmetry gate refuses reduction.

Let P average coordinate-row orbits and C average atom-column orbits. The reduced system is P A C x=P b. Every atom orbit consists of nonnegative generators, so an orbit coefficient expands to equal nonnegative coefficients on its members. Since the target and expanded columns are invariant, satisfying all averaged coordinate equations satisfies the original equations. Duplicate projected columns can be represented by one retained orbit. This is a symmetry argument within the coefficient search, not a new physical positivity axiom.

The 400 quotient coordinates reduce to 210 spin-exchange orbits. The 96,418 atoms form 48,342 spin-exchange orbits; removing identical projected columns leaves 33,762 distinct directions. The joint system therefore has 421 rows, 67,573 columns and 3,633,098 nonzeros. Exact feature checks and reduction took 63.87 seconds using the previously generated cache. The reduced native IPM solve took 44.87 seconds. These are single-run timings; the roughly 4.5-fold comparison concerns the LP solve stage alone and excludes initial dictionary generation, reduction and export.

Full unsymmetrized rational export accepted the reduced solution. Its certified metric floor is 0.000896925856 and numerator floor approximately 0.0000875508588826. The weight proof has 368 positive indicators and 52 charge localizers; the numerator has 308 and 18. Again, each proof has 1,364 number-multiplier terms. Both source hash binding and exact full replay are retained.

The small two-site tests cover fresh construction, symmetry reduction, orbit expansion, exact export and refusal of corrupt caches, with determinant actions, sector generation and the legacy full-population lift disabled. The final full-suite result and independent replay paths are recorded in the progress record.

## Remaining mathematical blockers

The H6 construction blocker is closed within this generated basis and atom dictionary. The next targets are a controlled larger-system test and useful accuracy with smaller generated dictionaries. The current quotient row budget already excludes an eight-site half-filled run at the configured degree, so the H6 result is not a scaling result.

The new gap proof also integrates with the existing accurate H6 energy certificate, preserving its approximately 7.41698×10^−12 interval width. That energy integration retains the old 20-dimensional reference, 17 response directions and 400-configuration witness; only the gap metric and positivity certificate were discovered from bare input. Bounded positivity degree must still be shown to retain useful accuracy as systems grow, and the energy response representation must avoid full configuration dependence. Neither this construction nor its symmetry reductions characterize the entire cone of physical two-particle marginals, establish a general efficient ground-state solver, or provide thermodynamic, kinetic or synthesis predictions.

Using the freshly discovered spin-exchange certificate as reference, the existing exact monotone-transfer verifier also accepts the 1/50 perturbation, the mixed-sign example, and the previously established conservative endpoint of the hopping family. The 1/50 energy integration preserves its approximately 3.92939×10^−12 interval width. These are transfers of the new reference proof, not three additional fresh metric searches. The three integrations and five gap checks give eight new independent standard-library replays.
