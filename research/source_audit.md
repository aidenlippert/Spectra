# Source and mathematics audit — consolidated receipt

Audit date: 2026-09-10. Independent source-review agents and the root inspected primary records and corrected the reports. This is a factual and mathematical scope review; it does not verify experimental realizability.

Material corrections retained in the final notes:

- **Thermodynamics:** a closed energy-conserving global unitary preserves global free energy exactly. At decoupled endpoints the balance includes all marginal free energies and total correlations. An initially Gibbs bath minimizes free energy at its initial inverse temperature; losing bath energy is not by itself entropy-free work. These are direct energy/entropy identities. The separate passivity theorem is scoped to its allowed dynamics. [Pusz–Woronowicz](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01614224).
- **Learning:** the finite-family bounded-event test gives a sufficient sample bound. A matching necessary rate needs an explicit product-distribution/divergence calculation. The finite simulator does not implement the broader causal-discovery theories it cites. [Polyanskiy–Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05616), [Evans–Harper–Flammia](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.07636).
- **Abstraction:** a row residual is bounded by the sum of absolute omitted Pauli coefficients; the vector bound is the Euclidean norm of those row bounds. Skew symmetry removes exponential propagator growth for the stated Hamiltonian coordinate system. This does not imply small dimension under arbitrary coupling.
- **Memory:** the [2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10943) is real and explicitly leaves TQO-2 open. The generic CSS checker supplies no proof about that construction. The [stability framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0344) has geometric and local-ground-space assumptions.
- **Control/synthesis:** delta-decision has overlapping tolerance-aware outcomes, not unrestricted exact truth semantics. Robust discretization completeness still needs an effective approximation modulus. [Gao–Avigad–Clarke](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.6671).

Bibliographic corrections: PRL 122, 020504 is by Bairey, Arad and Lindner; quant-ph/0302121 is by Schirmer, Pullen and Solomon; Massidda et al. is PMLR 213:68–87 at CLeaR 2023. Primary records: [Hamiltonian learning](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.020504), [controllability](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302121), [soft causal abstraction](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v213/massidda23a.html).

The exotic-target notes distinguish conditional models from devices: polynomial-size Deutsch-CTC PSPACE power; hypothetical nonlinear gates and their computational consequences; and the special coupled-boundary geometry of GJW. No source is treated as evidence of a physical hypercomputer, nonlinear primitive, laboratory wormhole or passivity violation. [Aaronson–Watrous](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2669), [Abrams–Lloyd](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.81.3992), [Gao–Jafferis–Wall](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687).
