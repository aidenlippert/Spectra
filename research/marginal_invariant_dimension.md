# Why the matched quartic coefficient space has 39 coordinates

This is a dimension argument for the **real-coefficient Hermitian, symmetry-invariant, finite-degree CAR space used in the matched model**. It does not describe unrestricted molecular Hamiltonians or the entire physical marginal cone.

Let there be m matched flavors, each with left and right fermion modes. Require conservation of the total occupation of each flavor. The local charge-preserving operator algebra has the independent basis

\[
1,\quad n_L,\quad n_R,\quad T=a_L^\dagger a_R,\quad T^\dagger,\quad D=n_Ln_R.
\]

Independence follows directly from its action on the four local occupation states: the four diagonal operators span the diagonal algebra, while T and its adjoint supply the two off-diagonal entries in the singly occupied block. These are even local operators; operators on distinct flavors commute. Products using different flavors therefore form an independent tensor-product basis.

Assign body weights 1 to n_L, n_R, T, and T†, and weight 2 to D. Body weight k means 2k ladder operators. Quartic factors produce squares of body weight at most 4.

After averaging over all flavor permutations, a basis is indexed by the counts of the five nonidentity local letters. Up to body weight 4, at most four flavors are occupied by nonidentity letters. Consequently this count is independent of m whenever **m ≥ 4**.

Real-coefficient Hermiticity swaps the counts of T and T†. The global left/right swap exchanges both n_L with n_R and T with T†. Imposing both is equivalent to imposing two independent pair exchanges. The bookkeeping ring for these count patterns has two generators of weight 1 and three of weight 2: sum and product for each exchanged pair, and D. Its Hilbert series is

\[
\frac{1}{(1-z)^2(1-z^2)^3}
=1+2z+6z^2+10z^3+20z^4+\cdots.
\]

Thus the real Hermitian invariant operator space through body weight 4 has

\[
1+2+6+10+20=\boxed{39}
\]

coordinates. This uses a commutative polynomial ring to count independent tensor patterns. It does **not** identify the physical local operator multiplication with that free ring; local CAR identities remain present.

## The truncated number ideal removes 19 directions

The invariant multiplier X has body weight at most 3, hence dimension 1+2+6+10 = 19. Multiplication by (Nhat - N) maps it into the weight-at-most-4 space and is injective there for m ≥ 4.

To see injectivity, take the component of a nonzero X with the greatest number k of nonidentity flavor factors. Here k ≤ 3. In (Nhat - N)X, the component with k+1 nonidentity flavor factors comes only from multiplying this component by the occupation operator on a fresh flavor. Such a flavor exists because m ≥ 4. In the associated graded space for flavor support, symmetrized distinct-flavor multiplication is the shuffle product, identified (after nonzero combinatorial rescaling) with ordinary multiplication of count polynomials. The fresh-flavor contribution is multiplication by the nonzero linear polynomial for n_L+n_R. A polynomial ring has no zero divisors. This top-support component therefore cannot vanish. Lower-support terms and -NX cannot cancel it.

The image of this **truncated** number ideal has dimension 19. The quotient relevant to this SDP consequently has

\[
\boxed{39-19=20}
\]

coefficient directions. This is not a claim that all relations on the physical N-particle sector are generated by multipliers of this degree. Additional sector-specific relations can exist, particularly near empty or full occupation.

## Computational check and remaining cost

The generator-based signed-orbit construction gives exactly 39 invariant rows at 8, 10, 12, and 14 modes. At those sizes the unaveraged row counts are 519, 1,531, 3,640, and 7,456. Timings and sparsity counts are recorded in `results/marginal_reynolds/coefficient_orbit_scaling.json`.

This removes factorial group enumeration from the coefficient projection. The present stabilizer splitting and Gram export still enumerate the full symmetry group. Moreover, a small coefficient space does not by itself provide a small or easily discovered positivity cone. The ten-mode result currently uses 2,082 scalar PSD variables to produce its tight certificate. Direct discovery has subsequently succeeded on the ten-mode case using a cubic base and scalar quartic column generation. Discovering such directions with controlled cost and accuracy beyond this symmetry class remains unresolved.
