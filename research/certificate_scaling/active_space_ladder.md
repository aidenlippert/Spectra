# Straight hydrogen active-space ladder

`active_space_ladder.py` generates distinct linear H4, H6, H8, and H10 chains with 1.4 Å spacing in STO-3G. Each fixture uses RHF canonical molecular orbitals, rationalizes the normal-ordered electronic Hamiltonian at denominator `10^12`, records an exact coefficient-L1 enclosure, and supplies a single-determinant Hartree–Fock upper witness.

The ladder is separate from the prior square and rectangular H4 experiments. H4/H6 FCI runs are optional validation controls only; they are not used by fixture generation or certificate discovery. The H8/H10 outputs intentionally stop at the inexpensive HF upper because full FCI is not required for this scaling input.

Run with the repository's existing PySCF environment:

```sh
.venv-molecule/bin/python research/certificate_scaling/active_space_ladder.py --fci-control
```

Outputs are written below `results/certificate_scaling/active_space_ladder/h{4,6,8,10}`. An optional `--localized` branch writes a separate output root and applies a one-axis Boys first-moment transform by diagonalizing the projected z-coordinate independently in occupied and virtual spaces. This block transform preserves the RHF determinant exactly and records overlap orthogonality plus exact CAR HF replay. H4/H6 outputs are in `active_space_ladder_boys`; H8/H10 are in `active_space_ladder_boys_large` without FCI. The localized representation has more retained nonzero rational terms after rounding, so it is a coordinate baseline rather than a compression result.

Aligned validation-only correlated uppers are generated by
active_space_reference_upper.py from integrals reconstructed directly from
the frozen rational H, including CAR normal-order signs. Use
active_space_ladder_references_aligned and _aligned_localized. Earlier
active_space_ladder_references outputs and the abandoned missing-sign/factor4
conversion are superseded; they must not be called aligned FCI controls.
The upper witness itself is always evaluated exactly against the fixture.
H8/H10 retain the largest1,000 amplitudes and include truncation error in the
reported bracket. Full FCI-sector costs remain validation costs, not evidence
of scalable upper-state discovery.
