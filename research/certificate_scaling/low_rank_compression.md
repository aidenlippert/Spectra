# Low-rank SOS certificate compression

This bounded experiment asks whether compressing a positive semidefinite Gram
matrix by eigenvalue rank is a better priced approximation than retaining the
largest entries.  It uses a deterministic (32\times32) PSD Gram with

\[
\lambda_i=(1+i)^{-1.35},
\]

and maps Gram residuals through a normalized linear operator dictionary.  The
reported `operator_residual` is the norm of the resulting coefficient error.
It is a proxy for the coefficient residual that must be computed from the
actual CAR dictionary in a production certificate.

It can load the accepted H4 degree-3 molecular fixture:

```text
python research/certificate_scaling/low_rank_compression.py \
  --fixture results/marginal_molecule_stress/h4_square_degree3_certificate.json \
  --output results/certificate_scaling/low_rank_compression/h4.json
```

That fixture has 25 charge blocks, with dimensions from 4 to 92.  The largest
blocks are not a full Fock-sector enumeration: they are the exported SOS
operator dictionaries and their integer factors.  The script reconstructs
each Gram as (F^T F/d^2), maps its residual through the repository's actual
CAR `gram_map`, and reports coefficient residuals block by block.  In this
fixture, rank-one truncation gives residuals as large as 2.37 in charge block
`(0, 1)` while retaining all ranks gives residual below (10^{-14}).  Thus
this real certificate does not exhibit a uniformly tiny rank frontier.

The `h4_exact.json` campaign additionally rounds each truncated factor to an
integer numerator over (10^8), reconstructs (L^T L) with `Fraction`, and
replays every CAR word generated by the block. For block `(0, 1)`, the exact
coefficient (L^1) residual is
`17900054010045261/250000000000000000` (about 0.0716) at rank one and
`1954979218381173/15625000000000000` (about 0.1258) at rank eight. The
reported `safe_scalar_penalty_l1` is a conservative triangle-inequality
penalty: every CAR monomial has operator norm at most one, so this coefficient
(L^1) residual is a valid scalar shift for a proof replay. It is a penalty
estimate, not an accepted lower bound until the full ideal and sector replay
is rerun.

Run the synthetic smoke test with:

```text
python research/certificate_scaling/low_rank_compression.py \
  --output results/certificate_scaling/low_rank_compression/smoke.json
```

`--backend cupy` uses CuPy for Gram construction and eigendecomposition when
installed; the CAR residual map remains a CPU sparse operation.  It is a
bounded GPU-ready batch kernel, not a claim of end-to-end GPU acceleration.

The two compression methods have different failure modes.  Spectral truncation
preserves positive semidefiniteness up to floating-point roundoff, but it
requires a rank choice and can still have a large mapped coefficient residual.
Entry sparsification has a smaller support description but generally destroys
positive semidefiniteness; its negative eigenvalue must be paid for by a
rigorous PSD repair or a residual shift.  Therefore a small numerical
residual is not a lower-bound certificate.

The useful structural condition suggested by this test is stronger than
"the Gram has a rapidly decaying spectrum": there must be a computable rank
`r` for which both the mapped residual and the proof repair cost are below the
target energy tolerance, uniformly over the parameter family.  Spectral decay
alone is basis dependent and does not provide that condition.  A certificate
implementation must replay the compressed operator from exact or outward
rounded data and certify the residual.

The corrected full-certificate campaign performs that replay with
`experiments.marginal_symbolic.verify` for rank caps 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64,
and `full`. All candidates were accepted on the H4 fixture. Their lower bounds
were approximately -35.44, -31.41, -13.29, -10.15, -7.28, -3.62, -3.36,
and -3.33 respectively. This is a finite certificate degradation curve, not
evidence of scalable compression. The earlier `h4_exact.json` is invalidated
because its standalone residual map omitted the left dagger; see its adjacent
`.INVALIDATED.md` provenance note.
