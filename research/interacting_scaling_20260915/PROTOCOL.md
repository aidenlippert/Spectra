# Interacting certificate scaling

The preserved transfer and NVIDIA campaigns are inputs and references, never
editable scratch space. All new successful and failed calculations receive
separate bounded process receipts. No cloud resources or external spending are
authorized for this pass. One heavy local calculation runs at a time.

The target is a complete fixed-even-N interval of at most 1.6 mHa on the supplied
rational Hamiltonian, including the nonsinglet screen, spin defect, orbital
rounding and any coupling allowances. Upper quality and every omitted term
remain explicit. No fragment particle number is imposed on the target state.

## Development and freezing

Existing H4, H8 and H10 are development/reference cases. A coordinate control
distinguishes exactly transported dictionaries from newly restricted local
dictionaries. The old family contains index-restricted pure triples and binary
spatial masks; neither is silently invariant under a dense orbital rotation.
Coefficient-L1 residual penalties are also basis dependent.

The direct constructor retains global operators of degree at most two and adds
spin-closed cubic dictionaries on overlapping spatial-orbital clusters.
Boundary blocks can span several clusters. Their factors retain all cross
terms within each declared block. Nested additions preserve earlier blocks.
The cold adaptation recipe starts with local widths 2 and 3, then adds width 4,
then width 5. Widths of at least 3 also enlarge the coherent collective block.
Larger blocks and the full construction remain available as separately charged
development choices when measured costs permit, outside the frozen held-out recipe.

Coefficient rows come from the Hamiltonian, actual declared Gram products and
valid ideal products, followed by exact spin closure. They are not obtained by
constructing and slicing a full global cubic map. Global number multipliers on
retained local two-body supports and global one-body supports preserve the
useful fixed-number freedom. No individual sextic contribution is required to
cancel separately. The exact checker reconstructs the complete remainder.

The fully coupled endpoint is the original input. Intermediate lambda values
0, 1/4, 1/2 and 1 are diagnostic Hamiltonians formed by exact partition of its
terms into single-fragment and between-fragment support. Coupling changes the
Hamiltonian, not the physical sector. A continued state/optimization is labeled
and charged as continuation, not cold discovery.

Before new H12, changed-geometry water and an orbital-space expansion are run,
freeze the adaptation recipe, geometry, model, basis and resource caps in a
machine-readable validation protocol. H16 requires favorable measured costs.
Forecasts stop allocations above the declared local resource envelope; they
are not mathematical impossibility claims.

Before any H12 input was generated, a supplemental capacity protocol was
recorded from the measured H10 peak (about 1.01 GB). An explicit initial
allocation-envelope refusal can trigger a fresh H12 retry with 4 million Gram
entries and 400,000 coefficient rows, with the same mathematical recipe.
Both 3600-second attempts are charged. This is a declared resource extension,
not a family-limit claim or deletion of the initial refusal.

The retained full reference is also given an 840-second main optimization
allowance in its extended fresh comparison, under the same 3600-second complete
pipeline cap. Its initial 450-second H10 attempt did not reach the automatic
refinement threshold and remains a charged attempt. A stopped search that has
not used the complete envelope is not evidence that the reference cannot succeed
with more of that envelope. These allocation declarations are separate from
changes to operator supports, exact constraints or physical inputs.

After the frozen H12 magnetic searches failed, a separate diagnostic was
declared to continue the saved local-cubic magnetic family in two 300-second
slices. This retains the failed frozen result and does not change operators,
constraints or input states. A further declared diagnostic tests the existing
mu=0.03 residual phase only if both slices fail and the automatic phase switch
has not occurred. It skips when that phase has already been exercised.
The initial diagnostic parent clocks together are capped at 2400 additional seconds,
including a width-4 main proof and replay if a magnetic screen succeeds.
This is a post hoc optimizer-allocation investigation, never a retroactive
change to the frozen transfer recipe. The frozen run's cost remains additional.
The declarations are `posthoc_h12_protocol.json` and `residual_h12_protocol.json`.
The residual phase passed its exact magnetic check. A subsequent initialization
error was repaired in `h12_recovery.py` by passing the accepted certificate through
the existing explicit source argument. The failed case is preserved, and the
retry uses only the remaining combined diagnostic allowance. This is a caller
repair, not a change to the frozen mathematical procedure.

After the main 450-second solve also missed the numerical target without entering
the residual phase, one final same-family continuation was declared with an
additional 960-second parent cap (300 seconds of solving, up to 600 seconds of
complete exact replay). This is an explicit extension beyond the initial
diagnostic envelope, motivated by the accepted magnetic-phase result. It requires
the preceding main proof to finish exact replay, retains every earlier cost and
does not count as a frozen transfer or matched-resource success. Its declaration
is `h12_main_refinement_protocol.json`.

## Acceptance and comparisons

Reuse exact CAR, spin-screen and MPS checks. An alternative numerical backend
may propose candidates; it cannot accept them. Exact rational orthogonal
orbital transforms pay their complete coefficient allowance on both endpoints
when both are obtained in the rounded rotated model.

Compare both algorithms with the same available optimized libraries on the
same declared local host. Count integrals, orbital design, state discovery,
map construction, solves and complete replay in fresh timed comparisons.
Charged conditional experiments are useful diagnostics but are not an
end-to-end advantage. Record failed searches, host memory, Gram entries,
coefficient entries, exported factors and exact proof size separately.

The proposed performance targets are 3x lower complete time or 5x lower peak
proof-construction memory at the same width. These are targets, not forecasts.
The earlier enumerated exact baselines remain visible comparisons.

The parallel singlet-trace work must respect exact ideal equations, forced
Gram null spaces and the L1-residual dual box before claiming an obstruction.
A bounded physical-model branch specifies one energy difference and separates
solver intervals from basis, geometry, correlation and nuclear corrections.

An accepted outcome is a measured interacting accuracy-cost advantage, a
reusable construction with demonstrated scope, or an exact obstruction.
Disconnected fragments, numerical near-certificates and failed finite searches
are not promoted to those outcomes.
