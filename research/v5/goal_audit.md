# Requirement-by-requirement audit

The active goal requested a **bounded demonstration** of compounding scientific capability, with mathematical guarantees, acquisition of reusable representations, sequential held-out transfer, fair baselines, resource accounting, adversarial review and iteration. It explicitly excluded interpreting caching, supplied structure or benchmark success as proof of unrestricted intelligence. The broad ten-keystone ambition is not declared solved.

The bounded milestone is supported by an analytic proof, exact arithmetic, numerical experiments and independent critique. The mathematical statement is not formally verified in a proof assistant or established as a novel research result relative to all existing work.

## Corrections made during review

- A first proposed frame/probe and a Gaussian-mean Bayesian model were inconsistent with the intended variance source. They were replaced before the reported run; current tests check all eight frame hypotheses and the actual likelihood.
- A nuisance quadratic relation is an inequality, not an equality. E[N]≥P(N≥1)+P(N≥2), not equality for unlimited-horizon policies.
- The none/A/B/AB complementarity comparison uses a common fresh target and strips B-only information down to its bit. Full mode vectors may encode both predecessor signs and are not permitted in that ablation.
- The acquisition correction is e+2e+2e=5e because B is acquired using potentially erroneous A. Four additional preparation terms concern the four target-risk bounds. These are not four physical transfers, and the certified margin is a lower bound, not the exact value of J.
- A draft contained an unverified source citation. It was removed and replaced with the authors' linear-SDE derivation.
- Stateful conventional ties, unbounded Gaussian amplitude, enormous relaxation time and unproved reset hardware remain explicit limitations.

## Remaining scientific ambition

The system has not learned the model vocabulary or invented its nulling procedure. It has not independently validated the physical instrument, delivered a useful material device, proved general compression of interacting matter, or established indefinitely compounding discovery. Removing supplied structure while keeping certificate validity, fair stateful comparisons and resource accounting is the next research problem. These broader ambitions must not be silently substituted for, or claimed solved by, the finite theorem.
