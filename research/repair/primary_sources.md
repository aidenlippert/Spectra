# Primary sources: representation repair and finite-data limits

This map separates established baselines from any proposed new construction. The sources concern model classes with explicit assumptions; none establishes that an unknown physical process can have its order, state, or coupling structure inferred from arbitrary finite input-output data.

## Exact constrained lumping (CLUE)

**Ovchinnikov, Pérez Verona, Pogudin, Tribastone, “CLUE: Exact maximal reduction of kinetic models by constrained lumping of differential equations,” *Bioinformatics* 37(12), 1732–1738 (2021); [arXiv:2004.11961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11961), [journal paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab010).** The algorithm takes a known polynomial ODE and user-specified linear output combinations, computes a smallest-dimensional constrained linear lumping, and preserves the specified outputs/dynamics exactly. The construction is algebraic and can reduce nonlinear polynomial systems. Its baseline assumptions are decisive: the governing equations, state variables, and admissible model structure are supplied. It is not identification of an unknown physical state from finite observations, and exactness does not transfer automatically to inputs or couplings outside the model.

The journal record gives the final pagination as 1732–1738 and DOI [10.1093/bioinformatics/btab010](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab010); this supersedes any preliminary metadata attached to the arXiv version.

## Approximate constrained lumping

**Leguizamon-Robayo, Jiménez-Pastor, Tribastone, Tschaikowski, Vandin, “Approximate Constrained Lumping of Polynomial Differential Equations,” CMSB 2023, LNCS 14137, 106–123 (2023), DOI [10.1007/978-3-031-42697-1_8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-42697-1_8), [open author repository](https://iris.sssup.it/handle/11382/560855).** This extension relaxes exact lumpability by a tolerance \(\varepsilon\), proves output accuracy of order \(\varepsilon\) under the paper’s model/domain assumptions, and gives a heuristic for choosing a tolerance subject to a desired reduced size/error. The bound is model-based: it does not certify unknown omitted physics, arbitrary interventions, inaccessible initialization, or feedback composition unless those are separately covered by assumptions.

## Minimal input-output realization (Ho–Kalman)

**Ho and Kalman, “Effective construction of linear state-variable models from input/output functions,” *Regelungstechnik* 14, 545–548 (1966), DOI [10.1524/auto.1966.14.112.545](https://doi.org/10.1524/auto.1966.14.112.545), [NASA bibliographic record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670049337).** The Markov-parameter/Hankel construction recovers a minimal state-space realization for a finite-dimensional LTI input-output behavior (up to similarity), when the required Markov parameters and rank conditions are available. “Minimal” means minimal among realizations of that specified input-output law; it does not identify arbitrary nonlinear, time-varying, hidden, or physically coupled systems from unrestricted finite data. In data applications, a model/order class and sufficient excitation/measurement quality are required.

## Predictive-state representation

**Littman, Sutton, Singh, “Predictive Representations of State,” NeurIPS 14 (2002), [proceedings paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf).** A controlled dynamical state can be represented by action-conditional predictions of future observable tests; the paper gives a finite predictive representation for finite-state systems and frames state as sufficient predictions rather than hidden physical coordinates. This supplies a useful baseline for history-dependent observable prediction, but does not by itself provide a finite-sample order-selection theorem or guarantee that a chosen test set is sufficient for a new physical coupling.

## Data informativity and the unfalsified model set

**van Waarde, Eising, Trentelman, Camlibel, “Data informativity: a new perspective on data-driven analysis and control,” *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 65(11), 4753–4768 (2020), [arXiv:1908.00468](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.00468), DOI [10.1109/TAC.2019.2950003](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2019.2950003).** The framework asks whether a finite dataset is informative for a specified property/control task over all systems in a declared model class that are consistent with the data. It gives necessary and sufficient conditions for several LTI analysis/design problems and shows that some decisions can be certified without unique system identification, while others require data informative for identification (often persistently exciting data). This is the right baseline for refusing to treat one fitted model as uniquely established.

For trajectory parametrization, **Willems, Rapisarda, Markovsky, De Moor, “A note on persistency of excitation,” *Systems & Control Letters* 54(4), 325–329 (2005), DOI [10.1016/j.sysconle.2004.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sysconle.2004.09.003)** gives the fundamental-lemma setting: for a controllable LTI system and input persistently exciting to order \(L+n\), a sufficiently rich measured trajectory spans all length-\(L\) trajectories. The dependence on the unknown state dimension/order \(n\) is explicit.

## Boundary for the proposed repair

These results support three baselines: construct an exact reduction when equations are known (CLUE); compute a model-based approximate reduction with an explicit tolerance (approximate lumping); or certify a task over all models compatible with informative data (Ho–Kalman/PSR/informativity). They do not justify inferring an unknown realization order from finite data without a declared degree/order bound, richness condition, noise/model class, or equivalent prior. A finite record can be compatible with systems of different orders that agree on the observed experiment and diverge under a later intervention. Therefore a repair experiment must either (i) use a supplied bounded model class and test informativeness for the consequential decision, or (ii) report the unresolved model set and refuse to claim a unique physical state/update rule. A successful realization on the existing thermal process would be evidence-driven state reconstruction within that declared class, not a novel claim that known realization theory has been rediscovered or that general physical coupling is solved.
