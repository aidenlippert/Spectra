# Continuation does not change the proof grammar

This pass selects additional combinations from the same 492 spin-completed
generators, retaining the same ten spatial Hamiltonian patterns and exact
tail. It imports the existing Model, pricing, integer direction convention,
exporter and exact checker without modifying them. The
[spin-completion proof](/Users/aidenlippert/Documents/Spectra/research/spin_completion_20260913/PROOF.md)
therefore applies without a new mathematical assumption.

Each rational direction matrix V defines odd linear/cubic operators B. The
rounded Gram root R is composed with V exactly, and each resulting D is
paired with its exact adjoint. The verifier reconstructs D^dagger D plus
D D^dagger, the quadratic baseline and the body-one number ideal. All
degree-six terms must cancel. The coefficient-L1 residual penalty and frozen
lower tail shift give an exact molecular lower. The independently replayed
rational Rayleigh upper is for that same molecular Hamiltonian.

The inherited 64-direction certificate is replayed before scoring a gain.
Its saved directions and generator references must agree exactly with those
in the certificate. Stored lower values and numerical duals are not trusted
as proofs. A changed source binding, generator or direction is refused.

Only accepted trials completed inside the outer budget may replace the
inherited result. A weaker result cannot replace the inherited lower.
The search preserves the strongest accepted lower, or the smallest sampled
sufficient direction set after an exact 1.6 mHa interval is obtained. The
target comparison uses rational arithmetic, including its equality boundary.
None of this establishes a globally minimal number of directions.

Numerically independent negative full-frame moment directions guide the
next candidate. They establish neither an exact full-frame obstruction nor
a physical energy bound until a primal certificate is exported and replayed.
Similarly, failure to find further gain or exhaustion of the time budget
is not evidence that the full completed-spin family is insufficient.

## Optional collective certificate for every unselected direction

The separate full-family diagnostic reconstructs every complete frame Phi,
including every a_i and Q_k^(s,t) a_i. If an exact normalized Hermitian
functional y has coefficient values bounded by one, annihilates all the
body-one number identities and makes the quadratic and full-frame moment
matrices PSD, then every selected moment matrix V Gamma_Phi V^T is PSD.
Applying y to any allowed SOS identity and bounding its residual gives

    every allowed molecular lower L <= C = y(H10) + ell.

Thus a full-frame feasible dual can be constructed from the small solve's
moments without solving a dense full-frame primal SDP. A numerical
generalized eigenvalue against the uniform fixed-N trace only proposes the
mixture size. The complete rational checker must accept the final mixture;
no floating eigenvalue certifies this implication.

If the previous independent physical certificate and its spectral residual
proof establish Lstar <= E0(H) for the exact same Hamiltonian and sector,
then E0(H)-L >= Lstar-C for every present-family lower. That additional
comparison is needed to call the gap a true energy-error floor. It is not
needed merely to state a ceiling or a minimum interval width with the frozen
upper. The independent physical proof is not a discovery input.

The separate-contribution control uses exactly the selected B basis with a
nonnegative diagonal Gram. The joint solve permits all PSD cross terms in
that basis. A difference between their accepted lowers measures the found
coupling gain, not an exact optimum of the separate cone.

No new many-body sector or wavefunction is enumerated. The fixed upper's
earlier determinant-amplitude discovery, inherited direction discovery,
all current rejected trials, exact replays, and failed earlier controls
retain their respective costs. This finite H6 calculation does not prove
general many-body compression, experimental accuracy or preparation of matter.
