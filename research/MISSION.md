# Spectra: constructive mastery of matter

The standing mission is to discover what matter can do, construct the preparation
and operating policies that make it do it, and expand that capability across
chemistry, materials and engineered systems. This charter adopts the user's
September 10 mission correction. It supersedes the earlier choice of
two-generation compounding as the project's endpoint. The existing active goal's
older wording is retained as historical task state; its compounding criterion is
now a supporting research test, not a replacement for this mission.

Given required behavior, the sought output includes composition, structure,
feedstocks, transformations, assembly, measurement and operation. Predicting a
property or producing a favorable simulated design is insufficient. A physical
claim needs evidence about the actual execution, including model applicability.

Three mathematical attacks remain connected:

1. Predict decision-relevant behavior across the interventions proposed by design.
2. Construct reachable preparation and operating policies under explicit budgets.
3. Discover representations, mechanisms and methods whose applicability transfers
   across different physical systems, including their coupling and history.

For each central result record the quantified physical domain, prior result,
missing constructive obligation, proof or counterexample, resource dependence,
and physical evidence. Small calculations check those obligations; their scope
does not become the scope of the mission. Solver speed and compounding are useful
only insofar as they support these physical capabilities.

The current common attack is trustworthy adaptive invention: turn physically
grounded evidence into an executable policy plus feasibility and performance
bounds, or identify the exact unresolved prediction/reachability/applicability
gap. [The mathematical work](mission/CONSTRUCTIVE_BRIDGE.md) distinguishes a
conditional constructive connection from discovery of a new physical capability.

All V1–V12 evidence remains preserved. The retired history-state candidate stays
retired, the measured-data ambiguity gate remains a validity requirement, and
V4 calibration remains outside V5's dependency ledger. The V12 cover preflight
failed its declared timing gate; further percentage optimization is not the
central next task. No universal solution, autonomous scientific-method chain, or
physical synthesis result has been established.
