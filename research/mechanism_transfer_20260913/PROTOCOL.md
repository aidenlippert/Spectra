    B[i,s,t,u; alpha] = sum[k,p] weight[k]^alpha L[k][i,p] Q[k,s,t] a[p,u].

matrices already in the Hamiltonian; all spin components remain available.

then freeze that choice before any new energy solve or held-out fixture
