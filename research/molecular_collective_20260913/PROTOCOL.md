# Molecular collective patterns: bounded pass

Extract real spin-summed density-square factors from the frozen H4/H6
Hamiltonians themselves. Reconstruct every fermionic contraction exactly.
Any mismatch to the frozen rational Hamiltonian is charged by a rigorous
coefficient norm. No previously discovered proof factors or physical ground
states are inputs to pattern discovery.

Certify the discarded coefficient matrix between two multiples of a fixed
density-feature metric. Use a particle-number/spin Casimir identity to bound
all discarded squares together, without enumerating the physical sector.
Measure the remainder-width frontier against retained factor count. H8/H10
provide input-size controls; ten spatial orbitals is the hard budget.

Also test a cheap solver for retained factors: linear supporting bounds for
their squares, retaining the resulting common one-body Hamiltonian exactly.
An exact feasible one-body density matrix provides a dual ceiling for this
specific supporting-bound family. Thus a weak energy interval can be
distinguished from a poor numerical search. This ceiling does not obstruct
other coupled-pattern methods.

Accepting paths use rational CAR identities and PSD checks, never numerical
eigenvalues. Full fixed-N Hamiltonians are separate validation controls capped
at twelve spin orbitals. All construction, proposal, optimization, and replay
costs are recorded. Small factor count is not equated with a small many-body
state space or general many-electron solvability.
