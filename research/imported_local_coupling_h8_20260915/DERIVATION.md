# Exact mathematical construction

## Orbital transport and full-spin bound

The rational Givens rotations define a real orthogonal spatial transformation R, applied identically to alpha and beta modes. Its second-quantized unitary U preserves particle number and commutes with total spin. We never construct the many-electron matrix U.

Let H be the uploaded original rational Hamiltonian and Hloc the rounded localized Hamiltonian. Directed integer intervals through one- and two-body coefficient rotations prove

    ||U H U^dagger - Hloc|| <= epsilon_rot.

The accepting norm bound is the sum of absolute coefficient enclosures; each ladder monomial has operator norm at most one. Every coefficient and rotation is included. Rotation is not integral screening or an omission of long-range terms.

For exact spin averaging T, contractivity gives

    ||U T(H) U^dagger - T(Hloc)|| <= epsilon_rot.

The localized singlet certificate has positive factors, global fixed-number identities, singlet-compressed-zero ladder identities, and a recomputed remainder. Therefore

    T(Hloc)|_(S=0,N=8) >= b - ||remainder||_1 = l0.

The inherited M_S=1 proof bounds all nonsinglet multiplets of T(H), because N is even. Let that bound be l1 and let delta dominate the checked original spin defect. The implemented checker conservatively chooses the maximum original/local checked defect. Then

    E0(H) >= min(l0 - epsilon_rot, l1) - delta.

The MPS upper is computed directly on H, so no orbital error is added to its endpoint.

## Local versus coupled positivity

Global degree-at-most-two positive blocks are retained. The cubic operator lists are generated on two overlapping six-orbital patches. Spin-1/2 and spin-3/2 highest-weight bases are generated by sparse rational kernels of the exact raising action. Real positive Gram matrices span all multiplicity couplings inside each patch.

The independent cubic blocks can produce degree-six contributions. They are not deleted. The global quartic number multiplier absorbs precisely the components permitted by fixed particle number; the rest must cancel or be paid in the actual remainder. The underlying identity for a normal-ordered two-body operator X is

    sum_r a_r^dagger X a_r = (Nhat-2) X.

On N=8 this becomes 6 X. Requiring the sextic part to vanish on the entire Fock space is a stricter condition. The successful experiment changes the factor relationship and the ideal freedom together; it does not isolate their individual causal contributions.

No local fragment charge is fixed. All original interpatch Hamiltonian terms remain in the reconstruction. The global quadratic blocks and global number/spin equations are important dependencies, not costless boundary corrections.

## Exact restricted-family ceiling

For the declared paired local family, let a real linear functional y satisfy:

    y(I)=1;
    y(all admitted sector ideals)=0;
    y(T(A))=y(A);
    y(B^dagger B + B B^dagger) >= 0 on each paired block;
    y(B^dagger B) >= 0 on each quadratic block;
    |y(w)| <= 1 on every residual word.

For any accepted coefficient-L1 certificate in that family,

    Hloc_spin = b I + S + ideals + R,
    Lloc = b - ||R||_1,

we have y(Hloc_spin) >= Lloc. Thus y(Hloc_spin) is a family ceiling, not a physical trial-state upper. Transport allowances are nonnegative, so U_original-y(Hloc_spin) is a conservative floor for intervals produced by this route.

The supplied dual checker constructs every moment matrix from literal rational CAR products. If a matrix A has exact kernel vectors whose deleted-coordinate block is invertible, positivity of its retained principal submatrix implies positivity of A. For that submatrix it checks an integer factor witness F and exact row-dominance of

    A_keep - F F^T / d^2.

No numerical eigenvalue accepts positivity. Missing block witnesses, incorrect null vectors, coefficient mutations, and wrong normalizations are refused.

The uniform singlet trace used as a reference follows from cancellation of S>=1 multiplets between the M=0 and M=1 traces of a spin-invariant operator. For arbitrary A, apply exact T first. With s spatial orbitals and N=2n, the singlet multiplicity is

    d0 = binom(s,n)^2 - binom(s,n+1) binom(s,n-1).

Fixed-M traces of canonical normal words are evaluated by occupancy combinatorics. This is not full fixed-N enumeration. The trace formula itself is not claimed as new mathematics.

## Exact pattern reuse

A normal ladder word of degree at most six involves at most six spatial symbols. Relabel those symbols monotonically to a compact canonical pattern. Spin raising/lowering and CAR normal ordering commute with this relabeling. It is therefore exact to compute the SU(2) projector once per pattern and remap its rational output.

All 56,557 coefficient columns and the selected independent row list match the original projector. Tiny independent bit-state tests check the trace and rotation arguments separately. None of these finite checks establishes favorable scaling of the molecular algorithm.
