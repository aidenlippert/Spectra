# Linked-cluster elimination: unclosed sufficient conditions

This route did not produce a certified U/t=8 construction. An earlier draft
used a Kotecky–Preiss-style criterion without deriving the needed activities,
normalization or coefficient bounds. That draft was not an accepted theorem
and is replaced here.

The familiar second-order charge-elimination calculation gives exchange
J=4t²/U. It does not by itself bound all higher orders or prove convergence.
A mathematically valid conditional tail statement is the following: if one
has actually derived connected interactions Phi_C and proved, uniformly in
system size,

* at most A B^(n−1) terms of size n contain any fixed site; and
* ||Phi_C|| ≤ J0 q^(n−1), with Bq<1,

then the triangle inequality bounds the tail above support size R per site by
A J0 (Bq)^R/(1−Bq). A finite-volume total-energy allowance still scales at most
with the number of sites times that per-site allowance.

Neither the coefficient estimate nor Bq<1 was established for this campaign's
fully coupled ladder. A heuristic bare ratio zt/U does not establish them,
and failure of such a sufficient estimate is not proof of physical divergence.
An exact local resummation would need its own derived residual bounds before
this route could produce a new accepted result.
