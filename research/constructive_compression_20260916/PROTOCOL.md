# Constructive compression investigation

The main objective remains a directly constructible compact many-body
calculation with a rigorous error certificate. A new finite full-amplitude
solve, a familiar formal identity or a conditional lemma without checked
hypotheses does not complete that objective.

Preserve the exact eight-site positive-cone interval as an achieved accuracy
result. Use the same open 2x4 Hubbard ladder, U/t=8, fixed N=8 as a correctness
test for removing dense many-body amplitudes from construction and replay.
The comparison total energy width remains 0.001t. This is not molecular H8.

Independent mathematical routes are local resolvent preconditioning,
linked-cluster elimination, local/tensor positivity and polynomial spectral
witnesses. Exploratory agent notes are unaccepted until their derivations and
claims have been reviewed. Known methods and current primary literature
remain the novelty reference.

The first concrete experiment uses the known PSD ground-eigenmatrix property
to guarantee overlap with an identity seed represented as a small charge MPS.
An inexact Chebyshev recurrence may then exclude lower eigenvalues using
scalar norm bounds, instead of expanding a dense matrix for a PSD check.
Every truncation and arithmetic error must be charged. Floating success
alone is diagnostic. Final acceptance requires an independently replayable
inequality and correct binding to the physical Hamiltonian and particle sector.

No global determinant list, full state, full Hamiltonian, previous state or
previous endpoint may enter the candidate constructor. Tiny or full-sector
enumeration may be used in separately charged model-validation tests.
Record tensor entries, bond sizes, all attempted constructions and complete
verification cost. Increasing bond is permitted but must be accounted for.

Use bounded local processes, one heavy process at a time. No paid compute,
installation, Git writes or modifications to previous sealed results.
