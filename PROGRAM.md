The long-range objective is a system that acquires a physical mechanism or representation, certifies its limits, and makes subsequent unseen engineering problems cheaper to solve. The immediate milestone is a reproducible research instrument and a defensible map of the missing mathematics. This milestone does not resolve the ten endpoint problems.

Nine parallel agents investigated the targets, designed experiments, implemented independent components, and challenged sources and claims. More agents are useful when they supply independent evidence; agreement between agents is not a certificate.

## What the first investigation established

The main experiment combines #9, #10 and #4 on a precisely admitted three-qubit family. Two previously absent interactions are introduced in sequence. Each stage is followed by a coefficient vector obtained by a declared deterministic rescaling and disjoint measurement-observable probes. The base Hamiltonian repeats across seeds; evaluations are not statistically independent physical systems. Exact rational observation-error inequalities bound the Hamiltonian error; those bounds widen energy intervals and propagate through dynamical prediction. All retained algebra and certificate assertions have independent numerical or exact checks.

The run demonstrates a working baseline. It does **not** demonstrate open-ended mechanism discovery, learned experimental strategy, or compounding intelligence. The dictionary and experiment bank are supplied. Ordinary support reuse explains the reduced greedy-search count; full-dictionary least squares is a strong conventional alternative. Every method pays the same sensor-record budget. Physical derivative estimation, shot cost and preparation error remain absent.

The scaling attack is more informative than isolated successful examples. A quadratic chain has compact closure through six qubits. Adding a ZZ interaction expands the five-qubit observable space from 45 to 510 coordinates. The six-qubit interacting run reaches its cap and declines to claim completion. The next useful theory must therefore describe when compactness survives coupling, rather than promise universal compression.

The memory investigation confirms a specific gap in the May 2026 preprint: its low-temperature model theorem does not establish robustness against arbitrary local Hamiltonian perturbations; TQO-2 is deferred. Our CSS diagnostic can test a finite local-generation obligation and return a witness. It has not reconstructed the recursive construction or proved anything about its thermal lifetime. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10943).

## Follow-up result: a bounded complementary-learning theorem

The second investigation produced [a constructive theorem and experiment](research/v2/PROOF.md): a learned physical response law enables calibration of a second law, and their downstream predictive value is strictly complementary. The general factor-chain theorem has doubling marginal improvements. Exact finite-shot bounds, all-action Bayesian certificates, and unused-control evaluation support the admitted construction. [Measured results](results/v2/REPORT.md) include the complete acquisition cost and an explicit conventional tie.

This changes the evidence for finite complementary transfer. It does not close the broader intelligence gate in the portfolio: the response class and source factorization are supplied, and strong stateful conventional methods can match the procedure. The separate quadratic representation-revision test detects two mechanisms and a cubic model mismatch but has no sample-cost gain. The source assumptions and finite domain remain substantive.

The next unfulfilled obligation is to combine representation revision with certified prediction in the interacting many-body setting while learning a reusable experimental or abstraction rule. A positive result must survive a matched stateful comparator, model mismatch, and the operator-space growth already measured. Definitions should remain separated: complementary knowledge value, ordinary amortization, learned research strategy, and broad scientific intelligence are distinct claims.

## Third investigation: a conditional way around exact-closure growth

The [local Pauli construction](research/v3/local_pauli_theorem.md) replaces exact closure with a certified approximation for one- and two-site Hamiltonians under strong known local depolarization. A coefficient l1 bound avoids the exponential physical-error conversion found in the Hilbert–Schmidt route. Complete commutator neighborhoods and rational propagation produce 109-coordinate representations for the tested 12-, 24- and 48-qubit chains, with uniform expectation error below 0.000239.

This resolves one restricted approximation obligation. It does not resolve representation discovery: the Hamiltonian, damping model and neighborhood-selection algorithm are supplied. The next required bridge is to acquire the relevant model/uncertainty and select or revise the approximation from evidence while preserving the certified gain under stateful comparisons. [Current active status](research/ACTIVE_STATUS.md) records what remains unproved.

## Fourth investigation: policy learning failed its compounding gate

The [V4 experiment](results/v4/REPORT.md) trained sequential action-value rules from solved coupled quantum design cases and audited them against exact Bayesian planning. The second learned rule did not improve on the frozen first rule across 32 held-out contexts. Exact signed-moment planning was stronger and cheap. This closes a proposed route to claiming compounding from teacher-policy distillation in this benchmark.

The accompanying [feature obstruction](research/v4/higher_order_obstruction.md) proves a missing-information result: individually identical action summaries can require opposite first actions. Learning cross-intervention structure from observations is now an explicit obligation. The early V5 parameter-count proposal was rejected under a fair baseline. The later executed construction below uses a different physical instrument and an analytic observation lower bound.

## The next coherent experiment

The next experiment should remove the unusually informative derivative oracle. Use finite-time unitary or open-system evolution, shot-based outcome distributions and explicit preparation/calibration uncertainty. Derive a bound on finite-difference bias and sampling error before selecting observations. Include a restricted experiment class in which some mechanisms are indistinguishable, so abstention has an operational cause.

Then compare representations that capture different physics: a closed quadratic observable algebra, a compact model with an explicit memory variable, and a general Pauli expansion with a strict size cap. Introduce couplings that preserve the first representation, couplings that require the memory variable, and couplings for which all admitted compact models must refuse the requested tolerance.

The learner should transfer a *rule*: which observable or memory coordinate to introduce, or which accessible experiment separates two explanation classes. Reusing a list of known supports does not meet that gate. The learning procedure must not receive simulator labels, hidden Hamiltonians or evaluation seeds. A strong conventional comparator should receive the same public vocabulary and acquisition budget.

The decisive comparison is on held-out coupled families after two successive discoveries, with acquisition costs amortized explicitly. Require equal confidence/coverage, no higher unsupported-claim rate, and lower total experiment or computational cost. Retain frozen, retrieval/refit, full-model and scratch-discovery baselines. Report a null outcome if the advantage disappears against a simpler explanation.

## Mathematical escalation

The easiest missing proof is not necessarily the most valuable one. Prioritize a lemma only when its conclusion supplies a needed physical operation.

1. **Coupling-aware closure:** bound representation size or truncation error in terms of the interaction algebra and accessible interventions. A finite invariant subspace theorem is elementary; discovering small sufficient spaces broadly is the unresolved part.
2. **Controlled memory:** for a genuinely dissipative discarded sector, derive and verify a usable memory-tail bound. Pure Hamiltonian oscillations do not imply decay. [The block-system calculation](research/constructive_lemmas.md) makes that assumption explicit.
3. **Certificate reuse:** show that a discovered operator partition or local inequality transfers while maintaining an absolute energy interval and reducing search plus verification work. Fixed greedy grouping is the baseline to beat.
4. **Identification under resource constraints:** derive separation for experiments the apparatus can perform, then charge the full cost of reaching that separation. Small descriptions do not guarantee accessible experiments.
5. **Refinement:** connect an admitted target requirement to a realizable controlled trajectory through uncertainty bounds. Do not promote the compiler until the intermediate guarantees compose.

## Evidence rules

Every retained result needs a domain, assumption set, construction, resource ledger, independent checker or experimental protocol, and a known failure boundary. Mathematical certification remains conditional on physical model applicability. A theorem over finite rational inputs must not be presented as a certificate of a real apparatus without a model-error bridge.

Separate existence, prediction, control and synthesis in the result ledger. Separate acquisition from reuse, absolute accuracy from density accuracy, nominal dynamics from model uncertainty, and simulation from experiment. A finite family success supplies evidence about that family; it does not establish an unrestricted theorem.

The first milestone is complete when the code, sources, certificates and failures are reviewable and reproducible. The long-range intelligence gate remains open. The most promising next advance is a coupling-aware representation or experimental strategy that earns its reuse benefit under the stronger evaluation above.
